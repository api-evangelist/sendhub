# SendHub

> SendHub is a business text messaging platform for companies in the United States and Canada. It provides bulk SMS, two-way texting, scheduled and group messaging, MMS, text keywords, toll-free and 10DLC numbers, shortcodes, hosted SMS on an existing landline, a shared team inbox, a contact database, analytics, and an SMS API. SendHub is a New Era Technology company.

Last verified: 12 July 2026. Pricing and plan limits below were confirmed against sendhub.com/pricing on that date. For current pricing, always check https://www.sendhub.com/pricing/

## Key facts

- Coverage: United States and Canada only. International service is not yet available.
- Monthly plans: Lite $19 (500 SMS/month), Lite+ $37 (1,000), Starter $52 (2,000), Basic $100 (4,000), plus a Custom plan quoted by the sales team.
- Annual plans: Lite $219, Lite+ $400, Starter $570, Basic $1,000, Custom quoted.
- Every plan includes local area code SMS, unlimited keywords, attachments, API access, inbound MMS, and included voice minutes. Basic and Custom add setup, training, and a named account manager. Custom adds outbound MMS and dedicated short codes.
- Trial: 14 days free. Approved 10DLC registration is required before the trial can send. Carrier fees are a one-time $5 for brand registration and $15 for campaign registration.
- Each account includes one dedicated text-enabled number in an area code of your choice. Additional lines can be added at any time.
- Contract: monthly or annual, cancel any time.
- Sign up: https://app.sendhub.com/signup/ | Log in: https://app.sendhub.com/login
- Support: https://support.sendhub.com/hc/en-us
