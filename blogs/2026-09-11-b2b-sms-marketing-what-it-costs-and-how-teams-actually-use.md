Quick answer: B2B SMS marketing uses text to move deals and accounts forward, not to broadcast offers. Lists are small and triggered, not blasted, so most programs run under $100 a month. The catch is that there is no B2B exemption from the TCPA, because the law protects the number, not the recipient’s job title.
